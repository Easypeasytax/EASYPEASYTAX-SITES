GST Interest Calculator in GSTR-3B: How the New Auto-Populated Interest Works

Understand cash ledger relief, Table 5.1 and the checks to make before filing your return.

EasyPeasyTax · Updated 6 September 2026
On this page

What has changed?

The GST Portal has enhanced its GSTR-3B interest calculation from the January 2026 tax period. It considers the eligible minimum balance in the Electronic Cash Ledger (ECL) during the relevant period.

Auto-calculated interest existed before this update. The enhancement improves the calculation; it is not the first introduction of auto-population.

Important: The portal figure is a system-computed minimum. Taxpayers remain responsible for assessing their correct statutory interest.

How auto-populated interest works

The portal uses the filed return and tax-period-wise liability breakup to calculate interest. The amount appears in Table 5.1 — Interest and Late Fee for Previous Tax Period in the subsequent period’s GSTR-3B.

For example, interest on a delayed January 2026 return is reflected in February 2026 GSTR-3B. Interest and late fee are separate liabilities.

Revised calculation and example

For a qualifying delayed-return case, the portal calculation gives eligible minimum cash-balance relief under Rule 88B(1).

Interest = (Net cash tax liability − Eligible minimum ECL balance) × Days delayed ÷ 365 × Annual interest rate

Here, net cash tax liability means the tax payable through cash before this eligible balance relief. Do not subtract ITC twice or use only the closing cash balance on filing day.

Earlier-period liabilities need attention

The enhanced Tax Liability Breakup table uses document dates in GSTR-1, GSTR-1A and IFF to identify earlier-period supplies whose tax is discharged in the current GSTR-3B.

If a January invoice is omitted and its tax is paid through March GSTR-3B, check the original liability period. Do not assume it attracts interest only from March.

Earlier-period omissions, wrongly availed and utilised ITC, and other cases may require different treatment under Rule 88B. The simplified example is not a universal GST interest formula.

How to verify or re-compute interest

  1. Log in and open Return Dashboard → Select Return Period → GSTR-3B → Prepare Online.
  2. Open the System Generated GSTR-3B PDF.
  3. Compare the tax periods, due date, payment/offset date, liability and eligible cash ledger balance.
  4. Where available, use RE-COMPUTE INTEREST in Table 5.1 and inspect the updated PDF.

GSTN issued a subsequent advisory about incorrect computations affecting some taxpayers for February 2026, where eligible cash balance relief was missed.

You cannot manually reduce the system-computed minimum. Increase the amount if your statutory liability is higher. If a discrepancy remains after re-computation, retain your calculation and raise it with the GST helpdesk.

Before you file

  • Confirm the applicable due date, including any notified extension.
  • Reconcile liability with the correct tax period and tax head.
  • Verify eligible ECL relief and the system-generated PDF.
  • Check whether additional interest is payable.
  • Pay interest through the Electronic Cash Ledger; ITC cannot discharge interest.
