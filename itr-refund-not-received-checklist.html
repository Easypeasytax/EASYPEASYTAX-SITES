ITR Refund Not Received? A Step-by-Step Status Checklist

E-verification, processing status, bank validation and refund failure: find the next step for your situation.

EasyPeasyTax · Updated 15 October 2026
On this page

Start with the status, not the waiting time

A refund claimed in your ITR is not confirmation that the same amount has been approved or sent to your bank. First establish whether the return is verified, processing is complete and payment has been issued.

Keep these ready: the relevant assessment year, acknowledgement number, filing and verification dates, processing intimation and latest bank statement. For FY 2025–26, select AY 2026–27.

This checklist focuses on individual ITR refund follow-up. Actual portal wording may differ.

1. Confirm verification

Check that the return is verified, rather than merely uploaded. The general verification window is 30 days from filing. If missed, check the delay-condonation process and consequences; approval is not automatic. Use an available verification method and save confirmation.

Official guide ↗

2. Read the return status

Log in to the official portal and open e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Filed Returns. Choose the correct year and view details.

  • Awaiting verification: complete verification.
  • Verified / under processing: check pending communications.
  • Processed: read the intimation and refund amount.
  • Defective / action required: respond within the stated deadline.

These are simplified descriptions, not an exhaustive list of exact portal labels.

Official guide ↗

3. Check the nominated bank account

Open My Profile → My Bank Account. Confirm the account is active, PAN-linked and validated, with correct account number and IFSC. Check nomination for refund. Correct failed details and revalidate. EVC enablement is separate from refund nomination.

Official guide ↗

4. Check refund payment separately

Use the official refund-status service for the relevant year. If payment is shown as issued, compare the nominated account statement and the payment reference, if available. If credit is missing, ask the bank to trace it and raise the unresolved issue through the portal.

If no payment record is available, return to the ITR processing details. Do not assume the refund was rejected.

Official guide ↗

5. Use reissue for a failed payment

When the refund has failed, fix the reported bank issue first. Open Services → Refund Reissue, create the available request, select the relevant refund and eligible validated account, complete submission and verification, and retain the reference.

Reissue is not a shortcut for a return still under processing.

Official guide ↗

6. Review notices and adjustments

Check Pending Actions → Response to Outstanding Demand and official communications. A proposed adjustment requires you to examine the demand and respond by the deadline shown. Do not agree to a disputed demand just to release the refund.

If processing allowed less refund than claimed, compare income, deductions and tax credits in the intimation. A correction or challenge may be needed; refund reissue does not change the computation.

7. Escalate an unexplained delay

If the above checks do not explain the delay, use the portal’s grievance facility or official helpdesk. Include the year, acknowledgement, current status, relevant dates and earlier request reference. Keep your description factual and save the grievance number.

Suggested description: “My return for AY [year], acknowledgement [number], was verified on [date]. The current status is [status]. My nominated account shows [validation status]. Please clarify the pending action. Earlier request: [reference, if any].”

No fixed refund date is promised here. Do not file a revised return solely to try to speed up processing.
